# GENCG H2501

This site is my course journal for Generative Computer Graphics (Fall 2025). It documents how my work moved from simple rule-based sketches to a final 3D system in p5.js (WEBGL). The focus throughout is not photorealism, but structure: how clear constraints, repeated operations, and controlled variation can produce complex visuals.

Across the weeks, I explored different ways to think algorithmically. Early exercises start with basic randomness and repetition. Later weeks introduce grids, transformations, timing, and autonomous systems that evolve on their own. A recurring theme is balancing stability and change: keeping the overall composition readable, while letting small details shift over time.

## What you will find
- **Rule-based composition:** systems that generate form from simple instructions  
- **Loops and grids:** repetition as a design tool rather than a technical trick  
- **Transformation:** rotation, scaling, and parameter mapping to shape variation  
- **Time and behaviour:** sketches that react to time changes and motion logic  
- **Controlled randomness:** reproducible “identity” through seeds and constraints  
- **WEBGL structure:** translating the same ideas into 3D space
